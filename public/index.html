Definitions matter

The director's 93% is completed within SLA divided by completed requests in the coordinator log. It is not requests completed divided by assigned. The separate 92% completion-rate target has an unspecified denominator.

Monthly SLA percentages have an undocumented denominator and cannot yet be reliably reconciled with coordinator YTD SLA.
